1 Overview

At BoostBangla, customer satisfaction is our priority. However, due to the nature of digital services, refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis. This Refund Policy outlines when refunds may be issued and the process for requesting them.

Important Note: Our services are delivered through automated systems. Once an order is placed and processing begins, it cannot be cancelled or refunded in most cases.

2 Eligibility for Refund

Eligible for Refund

  • ✓ Order not delivered within 7 days
  • ✓ Wrong service delivered
  • ✓ Duplicate charge on same order
  • ✓ Technical error on our platform
  • ✓ Service not as described

Not Eligible for Refund

  • ✗ Order already completed successfully
  • ✗ Wrong link provided by customer
  • ✗ Customer deleted post/video
  • ✗ Refund requested after 15 days
  • ✗ No Refill (NR) services
  • ✗ Multiple orders on same link

3 Non-Refundable Situations

The following situations do NOT qualify for refunds:

  • Completed Orders: Once an order shows "Completed" status, no refund will be issued
  • Wrong Link: If you provided an incorrect link, the order will be considered complete
  • Deleted Content: If you delete the post/video after ordering, the order is complete
  • NR Services: "No Refill" services have no refund or refill guarantee
  • Partial Refund Taken: If you accept a partial refund, you forfeit future refills/refunds
  • Duplicate Orders: Multiple orders on the same link at the same time
  • Organic Activity: If you gain organic engagement during delivery, results may merge

4 Refund Timeframes

Refund Request Processing 3-5 business days
Refund to Balance (Wallet) Within 24 hours
Original Payment Method 5-10 business days
Order Cancellation Request 3-10 business days

5 How to Request a Refund

1

Submit a Ticket

Go to your dashboard → Support Tickets → Create New Ticket with subject "Refund Request"

2

Provide Details

Include Order ID, reason for refund, and any supporting evidence (screenshots)

3

Wait for Review

Our team will review your request within 3-5 business days

4

Receive Decision

You will be notified of approval or denial via email and ticket update

6 Partial Refunds

In certain circumstances, we may offer partial refunds:

  • Order delivered partially (e.g., 500/1000 followers delivered)
  • Service quality issues affecting part of the order
  • Delay in delivery causing partial impact

Important: Accepting a partial refund means you forfeit any future refill or refund rights for that order.

7 Chargebacks

We take chargebacks seriously. If you initiate a chargeback:

  • Your account will be immediately suspended
  • All pending orders will be cancelled
  • Your balance will be forfeited
  • You may be banned from future services
  • Legal action may be pursued for fraudulent chargebacks

Please contact us first before filing a chargeback. Most issues can be resolved directly.

8 Refill Guarantee

Services marked with "R" (Refill Guarantee) include refill protection:

  • If followers/likes drop within 30 days, request a refill
  • Refill requests take 3-7 days to process
  • Services marked "NR" (No Refill) have no refill guarantee
  • Start count changes after order may affect refill eligibility
  • Maximum 2 refill requests per order

Note: Refill guarantee is revoked if start count goes down after order completion.

9 Dispute Resolution

If you disagree with our refund decision:

  • Escalate the ticket to a supervisor
  • Provide additional evidence
  • Request a manager review (3-5 days)
  • Final decision will be communicated in writing

All decisions are final and binding. Continued disputes may result in account termination.
