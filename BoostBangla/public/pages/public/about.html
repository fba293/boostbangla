Who We Are

Bangladesh's Leading SMM Panel

BoostBangla was founded with one clear goal: to give Bangladeshi businesses, creators, and marketers access to professional social media marketing services at prices that actually make sense for our market.

We saw too many local users struggling with foreign SMM panels that charged in dollars, had no Bangla support, and offered no way to pay with bKash or Nagad. So we built a better solution.

Today, BoostBangla serves over 77,770+ active users from every district in Bangladesh and beyond, processing millions of orders with a customer-first experience.
